# Nítido demo, 75 seconds, no voice-over, captions in the app only

0:00 Welcome card. Click Share. Pick the TextEdit window with 11 pt text.
0:08 The window appears 1:1. Zoom the recording on the strip: capture 60, display 60, latency single digits.
0:18 ⌘L. Codec HEVC appears, bitrate shows. Drag the divider across a line of text at 40 Mbps: no difference.
0:28 Slide quality to 2 Mbps: the right side smears, bitrate follows. Back to 30.
0:38 Second Mac (or second window): View, the name appears, type the code, connected. Strip shows rtt and glass-to-glass.
0:50 Draw on the viewer: cream stroke on the sharer. Draw on the sharer: cream on the viewer. ⌘Z on the viewer removes its last stroke on both.
1:02 Turn Wi-Fi off on the viewer. Reconnecting… attempt 1, 2. Turn it on: picture back, strip shows the gap.
1:12 Welcome card again, credit line. End.
